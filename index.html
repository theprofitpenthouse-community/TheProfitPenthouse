Inside the community, you move at your own pace through a clear progression — from mastering market structure to passing a funded challenge and trading live capital with firm money.

Master Market Structure

Start here. Learn how markets actually move — price action, structure, and the logic behind every setup. We build your foundation before you ever risk real money. No guessing, no gambling.

Once your skill and discipline are solid, you pursue a funded account challenge — where a firm puts up the capital and you keep the profits. This is where consistent traders get rewarded without risking their own money.
